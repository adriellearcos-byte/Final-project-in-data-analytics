# Final Project in Data Analytics: SDG 6 Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains the final project for our Data Analytics course. The primary focus of this study is **UN Sustainable Development Goal 6 (SDG 6): Clean Water and Sanitation**. 

Our objective is to leverage data analytics methodologies to analyze global, regional, or localized data regarding access to safe drinking water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene. Through this project, we aim to uncover trends, identify critical gaps, and propose data-driven solutions to support the progress toward achieving clean water and sanitation for all.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
* **Data Exploration:** Analyze key indicators of SDG 6 (e.g., safely managed drinking water services, wastewater treatment, water scarcity).
* **Trend Analysis:** Identify historical patterns and predict future trajectories of water quality and accessibility.
* **Geospatial Insights:** Visualize regional disparities in sanitation and clean water access.
* **Actionable Insights:** Provide data-backed recommendations for policymakers and stakeholders to accelerate SDG 6 progress.

---

## 📊 Dataset Information
